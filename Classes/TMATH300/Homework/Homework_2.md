Let $x=\{ a,b \}$.  Then $xRx$ is true as $ab=ab$. This makes $X$ reflexive.
Let $x=\{ a,b \},y=\{ c,d \}$.  $xRy$ implies $yRx$ as $ab=cd$ is the same as $cb=da$. 
Let $x=\{ a,b \},y=\{ c,d \},z=\{ e,f \}$. $xRy$ and $yRz$ implies $xRz$ as if $ad=bc$ and $cf=de$ then $af=be$.
As $R$ is reflexive, symmetric, and transitive, it is an equivalence statement.
